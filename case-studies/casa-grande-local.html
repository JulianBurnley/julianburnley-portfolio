Casa Grande Local

A directory-first community guide designed around useful local information, fair business representation, accessible discovery, and a realistic path from a focused launch to a sustainable civic resource.

My role
Product strategy, information architecture, WordPress and HivePress implementation, content design, launch, and operations
Primary audience
Casa Grande residents searching for local businesses and services
Secondary audience
Visitors and local business owners
Launch collection
Eight researched listings across four core categories
Platform
WordPress with HivePress as the directory engine
Status
Live at casagrandelocal.com

The opportunity

Local information is often fragmented across national search platforms, social-media groups, outdated business pages, and event posts that disappear quickly. The product opportunity is not to reproduce every possible local listing. It is to make a smaller set of useful Casa Grande information easier to find, understand, and trust.

Product decision

The first release is a business-and-services directory rather than a news publication or full events platform. That narrower scope is easier to verify, moderate, and maintain. It also gives residents an immediate task the product can perform well: finding a local place that meets a current need.

Audience and core journeys

  • Residents search by keyword or browse a familiar category, compare practical details, and contact or visit a business.
  • Visitors discover local alternatives without needing prior knowledge of the city.
  • Business owners submit a listing for moderation or report information that needs correction.

First-version features

  • Keyword and category search
  • Four launch categories: food and drink, home and auto, shopping, and community
  • Practical listing fields for addresses, hours, phone numbers, websites, email, and reported accessibility features
  • Moderated business submissions and a public correction process
  • Mobile-first, keyboard-accessible interaction
  • Public About, Listing Guidelines, Contact, and Privacy Policy pages

Trust and moderation

A local directory succeeds only when people believe its information. The launch plan therefore separates verified facts from reviews and promotion. Reviews should not launch without moderation rules, and paid placement should never be presented as an editorial recommendation.

What is measurable

The first useful outcomes will be listing completeness, successful searches, correction turnaround time, approved submissions, and repeat visits—not raw page count. Those signals will determine whether events, editorial guides, memberships, or sponsored listings deserve a later phase.

Live outcome

Casa Grande Local is now a functioning public business directory with a branded homepage, four visual categories, keyword discovery, eight launch listings, structured business details, moderated submissions, reporting, privacy and listing-guideline pages, domain email, caching, and a responsive public experience.

The launch converted the original front-end concept into a maintainable WordPress operation while preserving its local focus, accessible structure, transparent moderation standards, and clear paths for residents and business owners.

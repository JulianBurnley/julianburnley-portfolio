My Reflections

All the concepts in the lesson were new to me. Transformations are a great way to modify not just the structure and content of a website in interesting ways, including 3D, allowing for more ways to design the site. Transitions make the changes smooth, and animation provide more instantaneous changes that do not depend on progression. These concepts are a useful enhancement that will improve the user experience of most projects.

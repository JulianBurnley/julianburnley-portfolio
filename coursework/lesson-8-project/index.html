Lesson 8 Project

My Reflections

Learning about how to present information other than text or images provides a different way to decorate a website. Using gradients, filters, and blending allows for creative visual effects without relying on uploaded images. Canvas also provides an alternative to create original content instead of copying. Advanced graphical elements offer new ways to display high-quality images and descriptions that work across platforms, including lower bandwidth and processing environments.

My Next Steps

Filters, blending, clipping, and gradients were the most interesting to me because they provide a means of creating original content. I want to explore these concepts more deeply. Reviewing the lesson again and using the provided resources will help me learn more, and I plan to experiment with these techniques in future projects.

A clear process

From business conversation to useful website

Every project begins with understanding your customers, priorities, and day-to-day operation. You receive a written scope before development begins.

  1. 01

    Introductory conversation

    We discuss your business, current website, goals, timing, and likely project fit.

  2. 02

    On-site discovery

    When useful, I visit your business to understand its services, customers, content, and real working environment.

  3. 03

    Scope and proposal

    You receive the planned pages, features, responsibilities, schedule, revision limits, and price in writing.

  4. 04

    Design and development

    I organize the content, build the responsive experience, and provide a review link for feedback.

  5. 05

    Launch and handoff

    After approval and testing, the site launches with practical documentation and an agreed support plan.
